The Epson SC F100 is a high-performance A4 dye sublimation printer equipped with Epson’s PrecisionCore™ Micro TFP Printhead, delivering sharp, high-resolution prints with consistent color accuracy. Powered by Epson Precision Dot Technology, this sublimation printer ensures vibrant image output with excellent tonal range and density. It supports multiple print modes to balance quality and speed, making it ideal for a variety of applications including mug printing, T-shirt printing, art reproductions, and promotional items. Its versatility also extends to signage, custom merchandise, and textile products, providing professional results even in compact workspaces.

Using a reliable 4-color UltraChrome Dye Sub ink system, the Epson SC F100 Printer offers outstanding print durability and color performance. This dye sublimation printer is engineered to reduce operational costs while ensuring long-term reliability. The resulting prints offer excellent resistance to light, washing, abrasion, and perspiration perfect for custom apparel and soft goods. When used on polyester fabrics, the output also meets the Oeko-Tex Eco Passport® standards, making it safe for T-shirt printing and other applications involving products for children and babies.

Best A4 Dye Sublimation Printer

High Quality: Epson uses a PrecisionCore print head and UltraChrome Dye Sub ink for high-quality imaging with resolutions up to 600 x 1200 dpi. Epson Precision Dot Technology allows for prints that exhibit smooth gradations, a wider range of colors, and minimal grain.

Simple to Operate: The control panel tilts and adjusts, featuring a color LCD screen and push-button functionality. It comes with a driver that allows seamless operation with popular graphic and photo editing software.

Easy to Install: This device is highly compact and can be mounted on a desktop. It comes with over 1 liter of ink and 300 pages of media. You can operate it flexibly using Wi-Fi, Ethernet, or USB networking.

High Productivity: The device includes a tank system and a tray with a capacity of 150 sheets, capable of printing an A4 page in just 65 seconds.

Low Running Costs: It has extremely low power usage, and users can replenish it with economical bottled ink.

Superior Reliability: The product includes a manufacturer’s warranty, extendable up to 3 years, with optional Cover Plus for additional protection.

Adaptable Workflow

Epson Sublimation Printer aims to replace traditional screen printing, providing a simpler, more cost-effective solution for short-to-medium production runs. It requires less labor, uses fewer materials, and generates less waste. The use of digital imaging makes content creation and editing simpler, leading to output that is more accurate and adaptable. The streamlined and productive production process enables quicker job completion and effortless replication at the touch of a button. This method is suitable for fabrics with a high polyester content and hard surface materials. Moreover, it applies to materials that can withstand moderate levels of heat and pressure using a polyester spray. Create diverse products such as fabric and photo items, metal signs, mugs (mug printing), phone covers, custom clothing (T-shirt Printing), wall décor, and home accessories.

Eco-Friendly Fabric Printing

 

Epson designed the DS Fabric series specifically to ensure reliable and cost-effective performance. The Epson SC F100 model comes with a Continuous Ink Supply System (CISS) that users can easily refill using affordable 140-ml bottles (Black, Cyan, Magenta, and Yellow). The engine has very low power usage and can also go into sleep or standby modes which helps to decrease operating expenses even more. Additionally, the printer is shipped with both ink and media included. It also comes with a driver that enables seamless integration with commonly used graphic software.

 

 

The Epson SC F100 printer is compatible with both Windows and Mac OS when connected to a PC. It is remarkably small and lightweight, occupying less than 0.15 m2 of space. The printer supports both wired and wireless networking options. It is designed to work seamlessly with a compact flat sheet or merchandise-style heat press. The printer includes a user-friendly multi-page input tray and an extendable output tray.

 

For a comprehensive overview of the technical specifics of this printer, refer to the provided product datasheet. You can also access additional support materials via the Product Support link.

Product Frequently Asking Questions

Is the Epson SureColor F100 a sublimation printer?

Yes, the Epson SureColor SC-F100 is a professional-grade A4 dye sublimation printer designed specifically for high-quality sublimation printing. Compact in size, it is ideal for small businesses and startups creating personalized items like mugs, T-shirts, phone cases, and promotional merchandise. The Epson SC F100 uses dye sublimation ink and transfer paper to produce vibrant, durable prints on polyester fabrics and coated surfaces with excellent color accuracy and detail.

What is the printing resolution of the SC F100?

The printer offers a 600 x 1,200 dpi (dots per inch) printing resolution, ensuring sharp and detailed prints.

Does the printer come with sublimation ink and paper?

The printer package includes a set of starter sublimation ink cartridges. However, sublimation paper is typically purchased separately.

Can I use the Epson SC-F100 for commercial production?

Yes, the Epson SC-F100 is well-suited for commercial production, especially for small to medium-sized businesses. It is ideal for applications like T-shirt printing, mug printing, and personalized gifts printing. With its compact A4 size, dye sublimation technology, and reliable print quality, it enables efficient short-run production while maintaining vibrant, professional-grade output across a range of products.

Does the SC-F100 printer support wireless printing?

Yes, the SureColor F100 typically offers wireless connectivity options, allowing you to send print jobs to the printer without the need for direct physical connections.
